Faucet & Sink Installation and Repair

Expert residential fixture upgrades and drip repairs for kitchens, bathrooms, and laundry rooms across Dallas, Oregon and Polk County.

Residential Fixture Specialists

Precision Installation That Protects Your Cabinets and Countertops

A leaking or improperly installed sink faucet can quietly leak inside cabinets, leading to rotted wood, mold growth, and water damage. At Level Up Residential Plumbing LLC, we treat every fixture installation with the care it deserves.

Whether you have purchased a new high-arc pull-down kitchen faucet, a modern bathroom vessel fixture, or need a stubborn leak repaired, we bring the proper tools, replacement braided stainless supply lines, and certified craftsmanship to ensure your fixture functions smoothly and securely.

What Our Faucet & Sink Services Include:

  • Kitchen Faucet Upgrades: Installation of single-handle, touchless, pull-out spray, and commercial-style residential faucets.
  • Bathroom Sink & Vanity Plumbing: Proper drain pop-up assembly, P-trap realignment, and angle stop shut-off valve replacement.
  • Leak Diagnostics & Cartridge Replacements: Rebuilding dripping cartridges (Moen, Kohler, Delta, etc.) to extend fixture life without a full replacement when cost-effective.
  • Garbage Disposal & Filtration Hookups: Integrating under-sink water filtration systems and disposals seamlessly.

Our Step-by-Step Installation Process

  1. Thorough Inspection: We inspect your existing supply lines, angle stop valves, and cabinet floor to ensure the surrounding plumbing is sound.
  2. Clean Removal: Safe disconnection and removal of old fixtures without scoring countertops or damaging cabinetry.
  3. Precision Installation: Securing mounting hardware, setting waterproof gaskets, connecting heavy-duty flexible supply lines, and verifying proper aerator flow.
  4. Pressure Testing & Cleanup: We pressurize the lines, run hot and cold water under full flow, verify all drain joints under head pressure, and leave your work area completely clean.
