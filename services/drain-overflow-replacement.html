Eliminate leaks behind walls and beneath subfloors with precision bathtub waste and overflow assembly repair and replacement in Dallas, OR.

Tub & Drainage Plumbing

Fixing the Hidden Cause of Bathroom Ceiling Stains

One of the most frequent plumbing issues reported by homeowners in Dallas and Polk County is water dripping through the ceiling directly beneath a second-story bathtub. The culprit is almost always a deteriorated bathtub overflow gasket or a corroded waste-and-overflow shoe drain.

Bathtub overflows sit behind the tub wall, subject to heat, soap scum, and shifting tub movement. Over time, original builder-grade rubber gaskets dry out, shrink, or crack. When water splashes up or the tub is filled, water escapes behind the finished tub surround and saturates drywall and framing.

Our Comprehensive Solution:

  • Access & Visual Inspection: Determining if the leak stems from the overflow faceplate, the tub shoe drain, or the slip joint connections.
  • Heavy-Duty Overflow Rebuild: Installing premium high-density silicone or sponge gaskets that remain supple and watertight against the tub curvature.
  • Tub Drain Shoe Replacement: Removing stripped or cross-threaded brass or plastic drain strainers and re-bedding with fresh plumber's putty.
  • Flood Testing: Filling the tub to the overflow point to thoroughly prove zero leakage under active water pressure before wrapping up.
